Curriculum Map

This course takes a Portuguese-speaking learner who already has the basics (he can read and write simple sentences) up toward a realistic IELTS General Training test. It uses a spiral approach: grammar and vocabulary are introduced and then revisited at higher difficulty, with an IELTS "drip" beginning early so the test format feels familiar long before the final sprint. There are three stages. Stage 1 — Foundations (weeks 1-6, A1→A2) covers present/past/future basics, questions, daily routines, descriptions, and everyday survival skills (housing, transit, shopping, workplace small-talk), with the IELTS drip starting as light familiarization. Stage 2 — Building (weeks 7-12, A2→low B1) develops opinions, comparisons, paragraph writing, and reading for gist and detail, with real IELTS-format practice on each skill. Stage 3 — Test sprint (weeks 13-16) focuses on IELTS GT strategies, timed sections, full mock tests, and error review.

Realistic outcome

A realistic target is IELTS General Training band 4.5-5.5, depending on study habits, exposure outside lessons, and how quickly reading and listening stamina build. The course is modular, so week ranges can flex: stages can be stretched or compressed, and lessons can be repeated or skipped without breaking the sequence.

How to read the table

  • IELTS element shows which test skill a lesson touches, or "—" if it is purely general English.
  • The IELTS drip begins in Stage 1 (first marked at Lesson 5) as light, low-pressure familiarization, then ramps up in Stages 2 and 3.
  • The student's own speaking is coached live by the teacher, not via these sheets. Materials are recognition, reading, listening, and writing only; no sheet asks the student to produce spoken English or practice pronunciation. Where "Speaking" appears below, it marks the lesson where the teacher introduces the speaking test format and question types for live oral coaching.
