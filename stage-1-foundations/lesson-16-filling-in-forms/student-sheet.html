Lesson 16 — Filling In Forms

Today you will listen and write, then read and write. Your teacher reads a conversation between a clerk and a customer. While you listen, you complete the form below. You do not need to catch every word — just the important details: the name, the spelled surname, the phone number, the date, the address.

formulário = form · sobrenome = surname · soletrar = to spell · data de nascimento = date of birth

Part 1 — Listen and complete the form

Your teacher reads the conversation twice, slowly. First time: just listen and follow the form. Second time: write your answers in the blanks. The first field is done for you as an example.

NEW MEMBER REGISTRATION FORM

Field Your answer
First name Lucas (example — already filled in for you)
Surname (spelled letter by letter) ____________________
Phone number ____________________
Date of birth ____ / ____ / ________
Address — house number ____________________
Address — street ____________________
Town ____________________
Reason for visit ____________________

Tip: for the surname, write one letter at a time as you hear it. For the phone number, you can write the digits in groups.

Part 2 — Listen and complete the notes

Your teacher reads a short message twice, slowly. Fill in the missing words.

MESSAGE FOR THE CLASS

  • The swimming class is on ____________________ (day).
  • It starts at ____________________ (time).
  • Bring ____________________ dollars for the class card.
  • The first class is on the ____________________ of ____________________ (date).

Part 3 — Read and answer

Read this completed application form on your own (do not write the answers until you have read it all). Then answer the three questions below in writing.

LIBRARY CARD APPLICATION

Field Details
First name Sofia
Surname Ferreira
Phone number 0188 445 730
Date of birth 12 / 07 / 1995
Address 9 Oak Street, Hartwell
Reason for visit To borrow study books

Questions — write a short answer for each:

  1. What is Sofia's surname? ____________________

  2. What is her phone number? ____________________

  3. Why does she want a library card? ____________________

Homework

  1. Listening at home: do the short listening your teacher sets from the ear-training resource (ielts/accent-listening.md). Just listen for the key facts — names, numbers, dates, addresses — not every word.

  2. Fill in the form: complete this blank form on your own, in writing, using your own real details. Bring it to the next class.
