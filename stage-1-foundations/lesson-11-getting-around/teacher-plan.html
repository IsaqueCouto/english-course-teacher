Lesson 11 — Getting Around: Transport & Directions

How to teach this

This lesson is practical survival English for moving around a new city: catching a bus or train, buying a ticket, and following directions on the street. It also gives a gentle taste of the kind of listening the student will meet in the IELTS General Training test, where a short announcement gives a time, a number, and a place all at once.

Remember the working method for this student: the student listens and writes; the student does not speak. You read every script and example aloud. The student's job is to recognize words on the page, listen carefully while you read, and write the answers. There is no speaking task anywhere in this lesson, and there is no pronunciation work.

Keep your voice clear and steady, especially for the listening exercise. Read each announcement at a calm, natural pace. You may read each one twice the first time through, then once more at the end as a check. Pause between sentences so the student has time to write. Give light Portuguese glosses only when the student looks stuck — the aim is for the student to start catching meaning from the English itself.

Objective

By the end of the lesson the student can:

  • Recognize and write the core transport vocabulary (bus, train, station, ticket, platform, and so on).
  • Catch a time, a platform or bus number, and a destination from a short spoken announcement and write them into a grid.
  • Read a short set of directions and find the correct destination, answering questions in writing.
  • Write four to five simple sentences giving directions from one place to another.

Target language

Transport

  • bus — ônibus (BR) / autocarro (PT)
  • train — trem (BR) / comboio (PT)
  • station — estação
  • stop (bus stop) — parada / paragem
  • ticket — bilhete / passagem
  • single / return — só ida / ida e volta
  • platform — plataforma / linha
  • timetable (schedule) — horário
  • on foot — a pé
  • get on / get off — subir (entrar) / descer (sair)

Directions

  • turn left / turn right — virar à esquerda / à direita
  • go straight — siga em frente
  • next to — ao lado de
  • on the corner — na esquina
  • opposite — em frente a / do lado oposto
  • traffic light — semáforo

Recycle: prepositions of place — in, on, at, near, behind, between, in front of.

Read-aloud scripts

Read these aloud for the Listen & Write exercise (Practice b). Read slowly and clearly. Read each announcement twice, pausing between sentences. The information the student must catch is in bold for your eyes only — do not stress it unnaturally, just say it clearly.

Worked example (do this one together, write the answers on the sheet so the student sees the model):

"Good morning. The bus to Riverside leaves at nine fifteen from stop number four. Please have your ticket ready."

(Answers for the example row: Time = 9:15 · Stop/Platform = 4 · Destination = Riverside)

Announcement 1:

"Attention, please. The train to Hill Park will leave at ten thirty from platform two. Mind the gap when you get on."

Announcement 2:

"The bus to the City Hospital departs at eight forty-five from stop number seven. This is a single journey. Thank you."

Announcement 3:

"Good afternoon. The next train to Green Market leaves at twelve o'clock from platform five. Please keep your ticket until the end of your trip."

Announcement 4 (a little longer, for the IELTS-style check):

"Attention. The bus to the Airport is now boarding at stop number one. It leaves at one twenty. The return ticket is cheaper than two single tickets. Have a good journey."

Answer key for the grid:

# Time Platform / Stop Destination
Example 9:15 Stop 4 Riverside
1 10:30 Platform 2 Hill Park
2 8:45 Stop 7 City Hospital
3 12:00 Platform 5 Green Market
4 1:20 Stop 1 Airport

Flow with timings (~45–50 min)

Warm-up (~5 min). Point to the Key language box on the student sheet. Read each transport word aloud once while the student follows with a finger and reads silently. Ask the student to write three words from the box that they already knew. No speaking — just reading and writing.

Present (~10 min). Read the Key language glosses aloud. Then read the four directions phrases (turn left, turn right, go straight, opposite) and draw a tiny arrow next to each on the sheet or on paper so the student links the word to a picture. Recycle prepositions of place by reading two example sentences aloud: "The station is next to the bank." / "The bus stop is on the corner."

Practice (~20 min).

  • (a) Match transport words to meanings. Do the example together, then the student writes the matches.
  • (b) Listen & Write. Read the worked-example announcement and fill the example row together so the model is clear. Then read Announcements 1–4 (twice each). The student writes the time, platform/stop number, and destination into the grid. Read all four once more as a check.
  • (c) Read & find. The student reads the short directions text, marks the destination on the simple described map, and answers the questions in writing.

Produce — student WRITES (~8 min). The student writes four to five sentences giving directions from the station to the library, using the sentence starters and word bank. The student writes only; you do not ask the student to read the sentences aloud.

Wrap-up (~5 min). Read the completed grid answers aloud so the student can check listening answers against your reading. Read the homework instructions aloud and confirm the student understands what to write.

Common mistakes & gentle corrections

  • "get on / get off" mixed up. The student may write "get off the bus" meaning to board. Gently show: you get on to enter, you get off to leave. A small up-arrow and down-arrow on the sheet helps.
  • "single / return" confusion. A single ticket is one way only; a return is there and back. If the student writes "two ticket" for a round trip, point to return as the shorter, correct option.
  • Preposition slips with directions. Expect "opposite to the bank" or "next the shop." The natural forms are opposite the bank (no "to") and next to the shop. Correct gently by underlining the small word.
  • Catching numbers in listening. If the student writes "9:50" for "nine fifteen," do not say it is wrong straight away — read that one sentence again slowly and let the student fix it. Catching numbers improves with calm repetition.
  • "go straight" written as "go direct." Show that the fixed phrase is go straight (or go straight on / ahead).

Homework

  1. On the student sheet, finish the error-correction exercise (four sentences) and the directions-writing task if not completed in class.
  2. Write a short note (three to four sentences) telling a friend how to get from the station to a supermarket, using turn left/right, go straight, and on the corner.
  3. Listen to ielts/accent-listening.md and write down any three transport or direction words you hear in it.
