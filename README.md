# English Course — Teacher

The teaching side of [My English Course](https://github.com/IsaqueCouto/english-course):
lesson plans, placement materials and the curriculum map behind the student
lessons.

**Live:** https://isaquecouto.github.io/english-course-teacher/

## Why it exists

A student sheet tells you what to practise. It does not tell you how to run the
hour: what to do first, where learners reliably get stuck, or what to cut when
the lesson runs long.

This site pairs a **teacher plan with every student sheet**, so a lesson can be
picked up and taught without preparation. 43 pages in total.

## What's in it

**Start here**
- **Curriculum map** — how the lessons fit together and what each one assumes
- **Placement check** — where a new student should begin
- **Teaching basics** — how a lesson is structured and why
- **Progress tracker** — following a student through the stage

**Stage 1 — Foundations**
18 lessons, each with a teacher plan alongside the student sheet.

**IELTS**
- Exam overview
- Accent listening practice

## Design rules

Two constraints shape every page, and both are documented on the site.

**The student never speaks in the materials.** Everything written is
recognition, reading, listening and writing — there is deliberately no
pronunciation content. Speaking is coached live by the teacher, in person, where
it can be heard and corrected in the moment. The sheets cover the silent skills
a student can work on alone.

**Everything is fact-checked.** Vocabulary, grammar explanations and IELTS
information are built to be accurate out of the box rather than approximately
right.

## Built with
